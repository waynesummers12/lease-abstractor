# SaveOnLease outreach links

Use these links in new outreach so Google Analytics can separate campaigns from direct visits. Keep the same link for a campaign and use a different `utm_campaign` when the message or audience changes.

| LinkedIn direct message to potential partners | https://www.saveonlease.com/marketing/referral?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=dm&utm_campaign=partner_outreach |
| Email to potential partners | https://www.saveonlease.com/marketing/referral?utm_source=email&utm_medium=outreach&utm_campaign=partner_outreach |

The referral page generates each partner's personal link with `ref` and campaign tags. Partners should share their personal link to receive credit for paid reports. Do not add names, email addresses, or other personal information to campaign tags.
