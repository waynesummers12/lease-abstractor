# Fictional CAM statement test case

This folder contains a **fictional** 2025 annual CAM / NNN statement paired with the user-provided PDF named `Sample lease used for demonstration purposes only.pdf`. The names, address, charges, and dates are invented. This fixture is for developing statement extraction and lease-to-statement comparison; it is not evidence of a real overcharge.

## Arithmetic the reviewer should reproduce

- Property expenses billed: $189,000.
- Stated tenant share: 3.2%; allocated charge: $6,048.
- Estimated payments credited: $5,400; stated balance due: $648.
- Four controllable operating categories total $95,000: landscaping $30,000, snow removal $18,000, parking $20,000, and janitorial $27,000.
- The statement gives a 2024 covered-CAM base of $80,000. A 5% increase would make $84,000 the conditional 2025 cap for those categories. The $11,000 property-level difference corresponds to $352 at a 3.2% share. This is a **conditional variance to investigate**, not a verified recovery amount: the prior-year base, category treatment, amendments, and cap calculations need supporting records.

## Lease-to-statement questions to flag

1. **Landlord general administrative overhead: $5,000.** The demonstration lease excludes this category from CAM. Its tenant allocation is $160. Request the general ledger and category mapping before treating it as a confirmed overcharge.
2. **Roof capital improvement: $12,000 billed in full.** The lease excludes capital improvements except amounts amortized over useful life. The statement has no invoice, useful-life support, or amortization schedule. The tenant allocation is $384, but the allowable amount cannot be determined from these documents.
3. **CAM cap: no adjustment shown.** Compare the 2025 covered categories with the $80,000 reference base and the lease's 5% cap. Taxes, insurance, and utilities are expressly outside that cap in this demonstration lease. Check the detailed prior-year reconciliation and any cap exceptions before concluding a variance is payable.
4. **Tenant share: 3.2%.** The percentage matches the demonstration lease. The underlying rentable-area denominator remains unverified; request the area schedule.
5. **Review timing.** The statement has an issue date but no receipt date. The lease requires audit initiation within 12 months after receipt and 30 days' written notice. Do not calculate a calendar deadline from the issue date.

## What a real validation pair should include

An executed lease and amendments, the annual reconciliation (including issue and receipt dates when known), prior-year statement or base-year detail, category-level charges, estimated payments, rentable-area schedule, and supporting invoices or ledger where available. Redacted copies are sufficient if the clause text, category labels, amounts, dates, and page order remain readable.
