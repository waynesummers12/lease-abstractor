# Fictional Summit statement test case

This fictional 2025 CAM / NNN statement is paired with the previously supplied `Summit Fitness Studio.pdf` lease. Statement figures and allocation details are invented for testing. Do not present the fixture as a real bill or its questions as verified overcharges.

## Arithmetic to reproduce

- Property expenses total $445,000.
- The statement bills 6.75%, producing a $30,037.50 tenant charge.
- After $28,800 in estimated payments, the statement shows $1,237.50 due. This arithmetic is internally correct.
- The lease states a **6.25%** pro rata share and approximately **4,800 rentable square feet**. The fictional statement uses **5,184 / 76,800 = 6.75%**. At 6.25% of the same stated expense total, the tenant charge would be $27,812.50, a **conditional $2,225 allocation difference** before checking amendments and the actual area schedule.

## Lease-to-statement questions to flag

1. **Tenant share mismatch.** Cite the lease's 6.25% share and the statement's 6.75% calculation. Request an executed amendment and rentable-area schedule. Do not call $2,225 recoverable until the billing basis is verified.
2. **Roof and HVAC project charges.** The lease permits capital expenditures, including roof and HVAC, to be amortized as determined by the landlord. The fictional statement lists $80,000 in such charges without an amortization schedule. Request project invoices, useful-life assumptions, year-by-year amortization, and allocation detail. Do not automatically disallow the charges.
3. **Management fee.** The lease includes management fees among permitted CAM / NNN categories and does not state a fee cap. The $25,000 property charge warrants a calculation and invoice request, not an automatic overcharge finding.
4. **No express CAM / NNN increase cap.** Higher tax and insurance amounts should not be treated as cap violations. Request tax bills and insurance premium support if reviewing accuracy.
5. **Dispute timing.** The lease gives the tenant 60 days after **receipt** of the reconciliation statement to dispute charges and includes waiver language. The fictional statement has an issue date but no receipt date, so no calendar deadline can be calculated. It also says the tenant owes any deficiency within 30 days of receipt.

## Why this complements the first fixture

The first fictional statement tests a lease with a stated CAM increase cap and excluded overhead. This one tests a mismatched allocation percentage under a lease with **no express cap**, broad pass-through language, and a short dispute window.
